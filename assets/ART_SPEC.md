# Prince movement prototype

The 32×48 cell gives the face room for a brow, eye, nose and side lock at the
480×270 reference viewport. It spans two by three 16 px tiles. Keep the collar,
belt clasp and planted boot readable at 1× before adding detail. Draw facing
right, with light from above. Use the same head and tunic masters across clips.

| Material | Swatches, dark to light | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ink | `#292334` | Contour and eye. Leave transparent space around the profile and boots. |
| Cape | `#422640` `#68314E` `#974862` | Dark burgundy-purple lining and a long split hem. |
| Chestnut and leather | `#573435` `#88503D` `#BF7950` | Group highlights into locks. Reuse sparingly for boot cuffs and belt pouch. |
| Skin | `#CE896C` `#F2C8A0` | Keep the cheek lit beneath the fringe. |
| Cloth | `#48536B` `#68869B` `#A4C9D2` `#F6E8CF` | Slate trousers, pale blue coat, ivory collar and trim. |
| Brass | `#D8AC66` | Small clasp accents. |

The transparent 448×48 PNG packs fourteen 32×48 cells left to right, with zero
padding. Pixel coordinates start at each cell’s upper left. The fixed pivot
(16, 44) is the ground contact line immediately below sole row 43. Feet travel
horizontally during strides and lift off this line in the airborne poses.

| Clip | Cells | FPS | Pose decisions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| idle | 0–3 | 5 | Fixed boots, one-pixel breath, hair lock and hem swept in a short breeze. Loop. |
| run | 4–9 | 10 | Alternate leading boot through contact, compression and passing. Arms counter-swing. The cape streams back. Loop. |
| jump | 10–11 | 8 | Compress, then tuck one knee and let the cape trail down. Hold the second pose. |
| fall | 12–13 | 6 | Open the arms and extend the legs as the hem lifts. Hold the second pose. |

Use lowercase snake_case clip names. Review new frames at the same scale and
pivot. AI-generated variations can shift face proportions or palette between
frames. Reconcile them against the shared masters before inclusion.

## Reproduction

From the workspace root, use Python 3 and its standard library:

```sh
python3 crates/asset_pipeline/tools/draw_prince.py
python3 crates/asset_pipeline/tools/draw_prince.py --check
```

The generator writes `assets/characters/prince_debug.png` and the 3× review
sheet `assets/previews/prince_debug.png`. The check compares both files to the
generated bytes and verifies that clip order, pivot and dimensions agree with
the manifest. Record the source, creator and license of every image in
`assets/ASSET_PROVENANCE.md`.
